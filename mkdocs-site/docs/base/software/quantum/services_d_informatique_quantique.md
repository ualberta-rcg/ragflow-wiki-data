## Overview

Our team of quantum computing analysts offers expertise, guidance, and training in quantum computing and hybrid classical-quantum computing. As of January 2025, Calcul Québec provides access to a 24-qubit superconducting quantum computer, [MonarQ](../../clusters/monarq.md), available via the [Narval](../../clusters/narval.md) cluster. See the [MonarQ](../../clusters/monarq.md) page for more details.

To get started with MonarQ, refer to [Getting Started with MonarQ](../../clusters/monarq.md).

**What is quantum computing?**

A quantum computer leverages the characteristics of quantum particles (qubits), including superposition of states, constructive and destructive interference, and entanglement. Thanks to these properties, a new computing paradigm is in place, promising greater parallelization for solving complex problems that remain intractable for classical computing systems.

## Applications

Optimizing complex problems is one of the most promising areas for quantum computing and can be applied to several research fields:
*   Machine Learning: The quantum computer could reduce learning times and processing delays.
*   Finance: The quantum computer could accelerate portfolio risk assessment and fraud detection.
*   Molecular Modelling: The quantum computer could allow for the simulation of more complex chemical systems and real-time reactions.
*   Meteorology: The quantum computer could allow for an increase in the number of variables for weather forecasts.
*   Logistics: The quantum computer could enable the optimization of logistics and workflow planning associated with supply chain management.

## Quantum Computing Software

Several specialized software libraries exist for quantum computing and developing quantum algorithms. These libraries allow for the construction of circuits that are executed on simulators, mimicking the performance and results obtained on a quantum computer. They can be used on all Alliance clusters.

*   [PennyLane](pennylane.md), Python library
*   [Snowflurry](snowflurry.md), Julia library
*   [Qiskit](qiskit.md), Python library

## Training

Our team of analysts offers theoretical and practical training sessions at beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels. These are offered online or in person at one of our university sites, in French or English.

The training courses currently offered are:
*   CIQ101, Introduction to Quantum Computing Theory
*   CIQ102, Introduction to Quantum Programming with PennyLane
