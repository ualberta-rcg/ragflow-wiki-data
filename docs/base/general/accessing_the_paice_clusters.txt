== For PIs ==

To run jobs or be granted large storage allocations (i.e. project and scratch) on any PAICE cluster ([[Killarney]], [[tamIA]], [[Vulcan]]) a user must belong to an AI project (AIP), and the AIP must have a tier assigned to it.

An AIP belongs to a principal investigator (PI).  '''If the PI is affiliated with one of the three AI institutes''' ([https://www.amii.ca/ AMII], [https://mila.quebec/en MILA], [https://vectorinstitute.ai/ Vector]), then their home institute should create the AIP and assign it a tier.

'''If the PI is not affiliated with an AI institute''' but is either researching AI or using AI in their research, then the PI should visit [https://ccdb.alliancecan.ca/paice/general_access_to_paice_systems CCDB -> Resources -> General Access to PAICE Systems] and check the appropriate box.  This will create an AIP and assign it a tier. 

To sponsor researchers in their AIP, the PI must:
# In the ''Resource Allocation Projects'' table on the [https://ccdb.alliancecan.ca CCDB home page], click on the RAPI to get to the RAP management page.
# At the top of the RAP management page, click on ''Manage RAP memberships.''
# in the ''Add Members'' section, enter the CCRI of the users you want to add.

== For users ==

Once your PI has added you to their AIP, you must [https://ccdb.alliancecan.ca/me/access_services request access] to specific clusters.  

Note that PAICE clusters may impose further requirements for access, such as registering an SSH key pair at [https://ccdb.alliancecan.ca/ssh_authorized_keys CCDB -> My Account -> SSH Keys].
